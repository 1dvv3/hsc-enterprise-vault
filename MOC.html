🚨 Trial Exam — Task 4, Week 4 Term 3 2026

80 marks · 30% weighting · 2½ hours including 10 minutes reading · ONLINE · practical AND theoretical

Topic (from the notification): Intelligent Systems, Data Science, Data Visualization, Enterprise Project "The Year 11 course is assumed knowledge for the Year 12 course." ❌ A.I. is not allowed in this assessment task.

Priority Unit Status
⭐ Intelligent Systems ✅ Named topic
⭐ Data Science ✅ Named topic
⭐ Data Visualisation ✅ Named topic
⭐ Enterprise Project ✅ Named topic
— Principles of Cybersecurity ⚠️ Year 11 — assumed knowledge, not named

Outcomes assessed: EC-12-01, EC-12-02, EC-12-03, EC-12-04, EC-12-05, EC-12-06, EC-12-07, EC-12-10

⚠️ EC-12-05 is "applies tools and resources to analyse complex datasets." Combined with "practical and theoretical components", expect hands-on dataset work — spreadsheets, filtering, charts, dashboards, possibly SQL.

Units

1. Intelligent Systems

Decision support systems and the three decision categories · expert systems (knowledge base, inference engine, user interface) · forward and backward chaining · evolution to probabilistic and learning systems · simplistic vs complex intelligent agents · inference techniques (truth maintenance, hypothetical reasoning, heuristic knowledge, fuzzy logic, ontology classification) · hardware (biometrics, haptics, touch and gesture, VR/AR, voice, microcontrollers, sensors, actuators) · computational thinking · flowcharts, DFDs and infographics · disruptive effects · IoT infrastructure · simulation and surveillance · decision trees and certainty factors · evaluating systems.

2. Data Science

Ethical, legal and social issues · software features affecting privacy · Privacy Act 1988 and the 13 APPs · Indigenous data sovereignty and ICIP · curated vs communicated data · quantitative and qualitative data · the four levels of measurement · structured, unstructured and alternative data · informatics · flat-file vs relational databases, keys, normalisation · SQL in LibreOffice Base · spreadsheets, pivot tables and dashboards · data scale and big data · sampling and storage · errors, uncertainty and limitations · primary vs secondary data · blockchain · data mining and machine learning.

3. Data Visualisation

The three purposes (simplifying understanding, telling a story, highlighting significant results) with model answers · software tools and the evolution of hardware · identifying patterns · OLAP — slicing, dicing, drill-down, roll-up · enterprise data warehousing · data integrity and bias · the five Vs of big data · interrogating a visualisation (interpreting, aggregation, filtering, outliers, reasoning) · dashboard design, visual hierarchy and UX evaluation · data security.

4. Enterprise Project

The four project phases · tools for managing and documenting development · the changing nature of enterprise · design and planning tools · development approaches (waterfall, agile, prototyping, end-user, outsourcing) · design thinking · risk analysis · hardware and software integration · training · the four implementation methods · the six testing methodologies · verification vs validation · reviewing organisational impact · a 28-question exam bank.

5. Principles of Cybersecurity ⚠️

Year 11, assumed knowledge only. Threat, vulnerability, risk and exploit · social networking vulnerabilities · cybercrime threats · the four breach attributes (confidentiality, integrity, availability, privacy) · legislation and frameworks · individual privacy protection.

6. Terminology

📖 Definitions only. Every term across all five units, A–Z, with the ten most confused pairs at the end.

Cross-Unit Threads

Top-band answers connect units. These are the highest-value links.

Thread Where it connects
Big data Data Science (volume, variety, velocity) → Data Visualisation (adds veracity and value)
Data warehouses Data Science (storage evaluation) → Data Visualisation (OLAP and EDW)
Privacy and legislation Data Science (Privacy Act, APPs, OAIC) → Principles of Cybersecurity (breach attributes, CDR, GDPR)
Bias Data Science (ethical issue, sampling) → Data Visualisation (bias in visualisation) → Intelligent Systems (bias in AI decisions)
Flowcharts and DFDs Intelligent Systems (communicating processes) → Enterprise Project (design tools)
Computational thinking Intelligent Systems (system design) → Enterprise Project (planning)
Testing Enterprise Project (six methodologies, test data) → Data Science (validation, data quality)
Machine learning Data Science (data scale, prediction) → Intelligent Systems (learning expert systems) → Data Visualisation (predictive analytics)
Structured vs unstructured data Data Science (types, informatics) → Intelligent Systems (IoT data) → Data Visualisation (variety)
Decision support Intelligent Systems (the DSS itself) → Data Science (the data feeding it) → Data Visualisation (the dashboard presenting it)

NESA Key Verbs

Verb What to do
Identify Recognise and name
Outline Sketch in general terms; indicate the main features
Describe Provide characteristics and features
Explain Relate cause and effect; make relationships evident
Explore Investigate, examine or study in detail
Investigate Plan, inquire into and draw conclusions about
Analyse Identify components and the relationships between them
Compare Show how things are similar and different
Assess Determine the value, quality or significance
Evaluate Make a judgement based on criteria
Justify Support an argument or conclusion with evidence

Relational verbs that carry cause and effect:

is caused by · leads to · results in · can impact · may affect
contributes to · helps reduce · protects against · increases · minimises

The response model that works for almost any dot point: Describe what it is → Explain its impact → Give an example → Recommend a strategy.
